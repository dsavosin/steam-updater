## Pitch

If Megabonk had co-op, this would be it: same screen-filling insanity, same
"how is this even allowed" energy, but built from the ground up around a
1-3 player squad. Your friends ARE your weapon: swing one around you like a
giant sawblade, launch another across the map like a cannonball.

## Features

Shipped or locked for the Early Access launch (July 30, 2026, $14.99):

- 1-3 player co-op; fully playable solo
- Switch the camera between top-down and third-person on the fly, mid-run,
  no penalty; nothing else in the genre lets you do it
- The squad is the weapon: tether to a teammate and become a spinning
  sawblade, launch a buddy into a crowd like a cannonball; hero kits are
  built around kinematic synergies between players
- 5 heroes at EA launch: Kitsune (fox-masked hunter who grapples onto a
  teammate and spins into a lethal energy chain), Chonk (living blob that
  steamrolls the horde, swallows enemies to grow, and can outgrow the
  screen), Frill (guitar fires sound waves, swaps between heal, DPS, and
  support depending on the track), Bonk, Fryball; 2 more heroes coming into
  the build shortly after launch
- 30-minute heist runs with randomized objectives: Sabotage (smash massive
  mining rigs into scrap), Escort (push the hijacked Silver-Sled payload
  through endless security bots), Hunt (track elite enforcers and tear the
  loot out of them)
- Screen-filling final boss caps every run
- 78+ augments, earned by completing objectives, that change how abilities
  work (a grapple chain becomes a gravity well or black hole, growth becomes
  infinite)
- 100+ stackable stat upgrades per run
- The Armory: between-run meta-progression; spend stolen Cold Silver and
  scrap on permanent power-ups, core stat boosts, and per-hero upgrade paths
  with new kit variations
- New heroes and skins unlock through in-game achievements and challenges
- 3 difficulties: Normal, Hard, Extreme (Hard and above add elite enemies
  with extra abilities)
- 12 run modifiers for extra rewards: Radiation, Elite Rush, No Respawn,
  No Heal, Beefy Bosses, Death Puddles, Suicide Bombers, Overdrive, Armored,
  Gun Ban, No Skills, Fear Is a Mind Killer
- 1 map at EA launch
- Setting: Koumen, a whimsical junk-punk sci-fi planet being bled dry of
  Cold Silver (glowing pink radioactive coolant) by the Adroa Combine; you
  are rebel Slampunks running corporate sabotage heists to break the machine
- Two-person dev team, active in the Discord every week

Planned during Early Access (roadmap; never present as shipped): heroes
Zoraya and Purrsecutor, new skins, daily runs with leaderboards; at 1.0:
heroes B-Hooper and Vexora, infinity run mode, a new biome and boss.

## Target audience

- Friend duos and trios, mid-20s to mid-30s, who run standing weeknight
  co-op sessions to decompress; the 30-minute run is sized for exactly that
- Survivors-like players who find the genre too passive and want something
  to actively do while the screen fills up
- They play: Megabonk, Vampire Survivors, Risk of Rain 2, Deep Rock
  Galactic and DRG: Survivor, Helldivers 2, Brotato, The Spell Brigade;
  watch Sineus: Arena Survivors as a new adjacent comp
- Streamers and clip-driven creators; the game is built to produce
  "how is this even allowed" moments on camera
- Validation: playtest phase hit 91% positive intent across 243 testers;
  "Megabonk with co-op" is the hook that converts

## JTBD analysis

Primary job: "I want to feel absurdly powerful and share hilarious,
high-impact moments with 1 or 2 friends."

- Shared power fantasy: the payoff is not "I got strong" but "we broke the
  game together"; augment stacking is the vehicle
- Weeknight ritual: a low-friction, high-laugh session for a standing
  friend group; short runs, fast chaos, no homework
- Story and clip generation: players are buying "you had to be there"
  moments as much as progression
- The genre problem it solves: standard survivors-likes play themselves;
  here players must actively slam, slice, and roll to weaponize each other,
  so the fun comes from what you do to and with your squad, not from
  watching numbers scale
